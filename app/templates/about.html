About KrishiSahayak

Empowering Nepali smallholder farmers with localized weather risk intelligence and live wholesale market rates[cite: 2].

Our Mission

Smallholder farmers across rural Nepal frequently face severe post-harvest crop losses and unfair market pricing due to a lack of real-time decision support. Operating without localized weather forecasts or transparent wholesale market price trends (such as Kalimati Market rates), farmers often sell at low rates or suffer crop spoilage during unexpected rain. KrishiSahayak bridges this gap through an open-weight AI agent ecosystem.
